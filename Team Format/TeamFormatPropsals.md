# Overview of Current and Proposed Team Formats in SWWU
Note that:
- 'gender' refers to the Archery GB gender categories, Open and Female. 
- Alumni are not included in any teams.
- Teams are made up of archers from the same club, one of the eight in SWWU.
- There is no limitation on archers being in multiple teams if they meet the requirements of those teams
- Incomplete teams are allowed throughout

# Current Team Format
## Experienced Non-Compound Team (ENCT)
- Highest scoring 4 non-compound archers
- Novices included
- No gender requirements

## Novice Non-Compound Team (NNCT)
- Highest scoring 3 non-compound archers
- Novices only
- No gender requirements

## Compound Team (CT)
- Highest scoring 2 compound archers
- No seperation between novices and experienced
- No gender requirements

# Proposal 1
Remove compound team and add unsighted team (UST) of 3 highest scoring barebow and longbow archers. Reduce ENCT to 3 archers to keep total number of archers across all teams constant. Compound archers are not elligible for any teams. Novices can be included.

## Proposal 1a
Rename ENCT to Sighted Team (ST) and allow a maximum of 1 compound archer into the ST across indoor legs only. Legs 7 and 8 would not count for the league. No compound in novice team.

# Proposal 2
Require that both gender categories are represetned in each of the 3 teams. Where a club does not have both gender categoires represented in archer elligible for that team, only team size minus one archers will contribute to the team. For current format comopund team this requires one female and one open.

# Leg 8 Note
In leg 8 archers shoot different rounds from the WA 720 family. Scores for archers shooting shorter rounds are converted using the handicap tables to an equivalent score at a given distance to ensure fairness when a team is made up from archers shooting differnet rounds. For ENCT this is WA 70m, for NNCT this is WA 60m.
